## IMPORTANT: How email delivery works

The form uses [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co/), a third-party service for websites without a custom mail server. The form is configured to send submissions to **martinhristov174@gmail.com**. It includes:

- Name, valid sender email, and message fields.
- Native validation and accessible status messages.
- AJAX submission, which displays success only if the service accepts it.
- An ordinary HTML POST action as a non-JavaScript / alternate submission route.
- A hidden honeypot field, visible links to contact directly, and a privacy notice mentioning the provider.

**It is not yet activated, and I have not sent a real submission to your inbox. You need to do the following once before public release:**

1. Publish the site or open its preview in a browser with Internet access.
2. Fill out the contact form with your own name, a valid sender email, and a short test message. Click **Send message**. If the AJAX request does not complete, select the displayed standard-form fallback.
3. Open the inbox **martinhristov174@gmail.com**, find the first-time activation email from FormSubmit, and **confirm your address**. Check Spam/Junk if you don't see it. FormSubmit requires this activation before forwarding submissions.
4. **Submit another test after confirmation**, ideally from a different email address. Verify that the message reaches your inbox, including its sender address and content. Only then should you announce the form as live.
5. Periodically check your spam folder. Like any email-forwarding service, third-party delivery isn't guaranteed for every message. Users can always use the direct email link.

The AJAX endpoint is in `contact.js`. The regular HTML form target is generated by `script.js` using the email in `site-data.js`. Changing the target email means reactivating the new address.

Never put a personal email password, SMTP key, or private API key in client-side code. Messages entered into the form go through FormSubmit (the provider), so review their terms and privacy policy when publishing for EU audiences.
